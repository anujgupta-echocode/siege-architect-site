Most siege games hand you a castle. This one hands you a budget and a grid.

Design the castle, then live in it

A 10×12 grid, four materials, one budget. Wood is cheap and burns. Stone is the honest middle. Iron shrugs off arrows. TNT is a decision you will regret at least once.

Fully destructible physics

Every block is a real body in a physics simulation. Walls sag, towers topple onto the units underneath. Nothing is a scripted animation — if it fell over, it fell over because of where you put it.

Six units, six answers

Archer, cannoneer, bomber, fire mage, catapult and frost mage. Flat arrows for exposed targets, bombs lobbed over a wall you cannot shoot through, frost that makes a block brittle for the next hit. Pick four per battle.

A 30-level campaign of real architecture

Every enemy is a different building, not a different health bar: a squat iron bunker, a tower on stilts, a TNT trap dressed up as a treasury.

Quick Battle

No campaign, no unlocks — pick a difficulty, get a freshly generated opponent castle, play a single match. Good for five minutes on a train.

Built for a phone

Landscape, one thumb, drag to aim and release to fire. Every control is finger-sized. No account, no login, no cloud save — the game plays offline.

Play it right now, in this browser

The full game runs in a browser — the same code as the Android build, drawn with the same procedural art and the same physics. Mouse or touch, landscape works best.

Open the web demo

About adverts. The Android app is free and is paid for by advertising: an optional video you can watch to double the coins from a battle, and a short break between battles. Nothing in the game is locked behind watching one, and no advert ever interrupts a battle in progress.

The web demo shows no adverts at all — it contains no advertising SDK and makes no ad requests. What the Android app sends, and how to opt out, is set out in the privacy policy.
